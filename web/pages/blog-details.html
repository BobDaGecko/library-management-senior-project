Staff Picks: Modern History Reads

Posted May 2026 · by Library Staff

Staff Picks History Non‑fiction

Our librarians are passionate readers, and each month they select a group of titles they believe deserve special attention. This month, we’re highlighting modern history books that explore pivotal global events through engaging and accessible storytelling.

These works offer context, perspective, and insight into how the world we live in today was shaped. Whether you’re a student, lifelong learner, or casual reader, these picks are a great place to start.

Visit your local branch or explore our digital collection to check out these titles today.

Featured Books

Modern World History

by Jane Smith

A concise overview of major global events shaping the modern era.

History Through Stories

by Alan Cooper

Engaging narratives that bring historical moments to life.
