Help & Library Policies

This page answers common questions about using the library, managing your account, and understanding our borrowing policies.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place a hold on a book?

You can place a hold by selecting a book and clicking the Place Hold button on the book details page. You must be logged in to complete this action.

How many items can I check out?

Patrons may check out up to 10 physical items and 5 digital items at a time. Limits may vary for special collections.

What happens if an item is overdue?

Overdue items may result in fines or temporary borrowing restrictions. Returning items on time helps keep materials available for everyone.

How do fines work?

Fines are calculated per day for overdue physical items. Digital items are automatically returned and do not accrue fines.

How do I renew an item?

Eligible items can be renewed through your account page, unless another patron has placed a hold.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. Guests may search, browse, and view details for all materials. An account is only required for holds, bookmarks, and account actions.
